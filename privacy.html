Privacy Policy

This page explains how the Spendly demonstration application handles information entered into the tracker.

1. Information you enter

Spendly may store transaction details you choose to enter, such as amounts, dates, categories, payment methods, income sources and descriptions.

2. Where your information is stored

In this version, transaction records are stored in your browser's local storage on the device you are using. The application does not intentionally send transaction records to a Spendly-operated server.

3. Your control

You can delete individual records in the tracker. You can also export a text backup. Clearing site data, browser storage or using a different browser or device may make locally stored records unavailable. Keep your own backup if you need to retain records.

4. Sharing and third parties

Spendly does not provide an account system or analytics service in this project version. Your browser, hosting provider or network may process technical information under their own policies.

5. Security limitations

Local browser storage is not an encrypted financial vault. Do not enter bank passwords, card numbers, account credentials or other highly sensitive information.

6. Children's privacy

This student project is not designed to collect information from children.

7. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as the project changes. This is an academic demonstration and does not establish a dedicated privacy support service.

This draft describes the current local-storage implementation. It is not legal advice or a substitute for a jurisdiction-specific legal review before any public commercial launch.
